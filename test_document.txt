Anthropic is an AI safety company founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei along with other former OpenAI researchers. The company is based in San Francisco. Anthropic created Claude, which is an AI assistant designed to be helpful, harmless, and honest. Claude is available through an API and through a consumer product called claude.ai. Anthropic focuses heavily on AI safety research and interpretability. The company has raised significant funding and is considered one of the leading AI research labs in the world.
